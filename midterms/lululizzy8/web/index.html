Play history

    Rule handbook

    The goal

    Be the first to play every card in your hand. The landlord (地) plays alone. The two peasants (农) are a team: if either peasant empties their hand first, both peasants win.

    The deal: two decks

    • 108 cards: eight of each rank and two of each joker. Suits do not matter.
    • Each player gets 33 cards. The 9 left over go face up to the landlord, who starts with 42.
    • Standard Dou Dizhu decides the landlord by bidding. This version has no bidding: the first deal's landlord is random, and the role then passes to the next seat each deal.
    • The landlord plays first.

    Card order, low to high

    A turn

    • Leading (nobody has played, or both others passed): play any combination below.
    • Following: play the same kind with the same number of cards and a higher main rank, or a bomb, or the rocket. Otherwise pass.
    • The main rank is the rank of the triple, the four, or the lowest card of a sequence. Extra cards ("kickers") do not count.
    • When both other players pass, whoever played last leads again with anything.
    • You can pass even if you could beat the play. The leader cannot pass.

    Combinations

    Revolution (this version's twist)

    • Playing a bomb of 6 or more cards starts a revolution: the order of 3 through 2 is reversed, so 3 becomes the highest rank and 2 the lowest. The jokers stay on top.
    • It applies to every comparison, bombs included (a longer bomb still beats a shorter one). A red "革命 Revolution" banner shows on the table while it is on.
    • Another bomb of 6 or more reverses the order back. A revolution bomb doubles the stakes like any bomb.
    • Strategy: with a hand full of low cards, a big bomb can turn it into a strong hand. Someone holds a 6+ bomb in about half of all deals.

    Bombs and the rocket

    • A bomb beats any play that is not a bomb.
    • Between bombs, more cards wins; with the same number of cards, the higher rank wins. So 3 3 3 3 3 beats 2 2 2 2.
    • The rocket (all four jokers) beats everything. One black and one red joker together are not a play.

    Scoring

    • The stake starts at 1 and doubles for every bomb or rocket played.
    • It doubles once more for a spring: the landlord wins before either peasant plays a card, or the peasants win after the landlord played only once.
    • The landlord wins or loses 2 × the stake; each peasant wins or loses 1 × the stake.
    • A deal that reaches 400 moves ends with no winner (it practically never happens).

    Controls

    • Tap or click cards to pick them, then Play. Play only lights up when the picked cards are a legal move; the message under the buttons says why not.
    • Drag a card left or right to reorder your hand (on a keyboard: focus a card and press Shift + ← or →). Sort puts it back in rank order.
    • Hint picks what the heuristic player would play. There is no time limit on your turn.
    • Other players' cards are always face down; you only see how many they have left.
