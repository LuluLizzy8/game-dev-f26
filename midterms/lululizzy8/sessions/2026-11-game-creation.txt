  What you see on your turn:
  You are seat 0 (peasant, you).
    seat 0 (peasant, you): 17 cards, last action: -
    seat 1 (peasant, teammate): 16 cards, last action: pass
    seat 2 (landlord): 15 cards, last action: 5 5 A A A
  Turn: seat 0 (peasant, you)
  To beat: 5 5 A A A played by seat 2 (landlord)
  Your hand: 3 5 6 7 7 8 J J K K 2 BJ ...

  Legal actions:
    1. 6 6 6 9 9
    2. 2 2 2 4 4
    3. pass

  How to move. Type the number of a move from the list, or type the cards, such
  as 7 7 or pass; order and capitals don't matter. If you type something that
  isn't allowed, it tells you and asks again. Turns where you only have one
  possible move are not played automatically for you; you still type it. The
  other players' moves print as > seat 2: 9 9.

  Rules in short:
  - Teams: the landlord (20 cards, plays first) plays alone against the two
    peasants, who are a team. The first player to empty their hand wins for
    their side.
  - Rank order: 3 < 4 < … < K < A < 2 < BJ < RJ (the black and red jokers).
  - Following a play: play the same type and length of combination, but higher;
    or play a bomb (four of a kind) or the rocket (BJ RJ), which beat everything
    else; or pass.
  - Winning the lead: if both other players pass, you lead again and can play
    anything.
  - Scoring: each bomb or rocket played doubles the stakes. At the end it prints
    your score.

  The game prints a list of every legal move each turn, so you don't need to
  know all the combinations to play.
